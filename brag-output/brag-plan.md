# /brag Plan: Nexus Multi-Vendor Marketplace

## 1. Project Concept & Pitch
- **What is it?** Nexus is a high-concurrency, production-grade multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace engineered with Next.js 16, React 19, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Stripe Connect Escrow, and BullMQ.
- **Who is it for?** Discerning buyers seeking heirloom-grade artisan goods (custom mechanical keyboards, studio audio, full-grain Italian leather, Olympic-tier velocity gear) directly from verified independent workshops.
- **What sets it apart?** 100% automated Stripe Connect escrow protection: merchant payouts are locked in neutral escrow until the buyer physically inspects and approves their delivery.
- **Tone:** `polished` (Restrained, architectural, crisp dark mode, high-contrast typography, premium sound design).
- **Target Duration:** 20.0 seconds (1920x1080 Landscape, 60fps).
